# Embryo Photo Edit

Embryo Photo Edit is Embryo-Zero's fork of [TOAST UI Image Editor](https://github.com/nhn/tui.image-editor) (2.0.0-431-g9ee993e, commit 9ee993e). It does quick photo editing in the browser.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
